# Withdrawal Flow — Message Script

---

**User:** withdraw everything

**Bot:**
```
Withdrawing *100.23 USDC*...

⏳ Pulling funds from Blend protocol...
```

**Bot (after on-chain confirmation):**
```
✅ *Withdrawal complete!*

💸 Sent: 100.23 USDC
🔗 Tx: 0xdef...456
⚡ Arrived in 4 seconds.

Funds are in your wallet. Come back anytime! 👋
```

Also triggered by: `withdraw [amount]`, `withdraw all`
